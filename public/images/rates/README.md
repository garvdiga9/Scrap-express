# Photos for the Live Scrap Rates cards

Drop a photo in **this folder** and it appears on the matching rate card automatically.
No admin editing, no code changes, no restart needed.

- **Formats:** .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp
- **Best size:** roughly 600x400 px (landscape). Anything smaller than 400px wide will look soft.
- **Keep files under ~200 KB each** so pages stay fast.
- Items with no photo keep their current icon, so you can add them a few at a time.

## Where to get photos

**Best: shoot your own.** Photos of your actual yard and materials look more trustworthy than
stock images, cost nothing, and are legally yours. Shoot in daylight against a plain background.

**Free stock alternatives** (all free for commercial use, no attribution required):

- https://www.pexels.com
- https://unsplash.com
- https://pixabay.com

Search the material name (e.g. "scrap copper wire", "aluminium utensils", "waste newspaper").
Check the licence on each image before using it.
